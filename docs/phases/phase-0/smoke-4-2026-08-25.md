## Ordering constraint discovered while fixing S2

Revoking a run's credential inside `abort_run` **breaks abort**: an abort lands at the worker's next status poll (§06-06), and that poll needs a live token. Revocation must follow the observed process exit, never precede it. The first attempt failed exactly one test — the abort e2e — which is the test earning its keep.
